Common Questions

Stump Grinding FAQ

How deep do you grind the stump?

We grind well below surface grade—typically 6 to 10 inches deep depending on the stump and your replanting plans—so grass grows smoothly without hitting wood.

Will your stump grinder fit through my backyard gate?

Yes. Our commercial grinder is designed to pass through standard 36-inch residential fence gates with minimal footprint on your lawn.

What happens to the wood chips afterward?

By default, we rake the fine grindings neatly back into the hole so it settles naturally—or we can haul the chips away completely if you want to drop topsoil and sod right away.
