Common Questions

Tree Removal FAQ

Can you remove a tree hanging directly over my roof or fence?

Yes. Tight-space and overhang removals are our specialty—we use technical climbing and rope rigging to lower every branch safely away from roofs, decks, and fences.

Is stump grinding included with tree removal?

Standard tree removal cuts the trunk close to ground level, and we can bundle full below-grade stump grinding right into your estimate so the whole job is finished in one go.

Do I need to be home during the removal?

Not necessarily. As long as our crew has clear access to the work area and we’ve confirmed the scope beforehand, we can complete the removal and keep you updated by phone or text.
