Our Story

Passionate Tree Care Experts in Macomb.

We’re a local Macomb crew that lives for the heavy lifting. Whether you’ve got a massive oak that needs to go or just want some precision pruning to clean up your view, we show up with the right gear and get to work. We’re also the first call for many neighbors when Michigan weather gets wild—handling storm damage cleanup and emergency removals quickly so you can get back to normal.

We specialize in the tricky stuff, like tight-space removals and high-climbing jobs that most guys won't touch. Once the tree is down, our stump grinding makes sure there’s no eyesore left behind—just a clean yard and better curb appeal. We’re fully licensed and insured, but more importantly, we’re neighbors who take pride in leaving your property better than we found it.

Tree Removal

Taking down a tree is a massive undertaking, but we handle the heavy lifting safely and efficiently.

  • Hazardous & Leaning Trees: Safe extractions near roofs, power lines, and tight structures.
  • Precision Rigging: Advanced climbing and rope techniques ensure a controlled descent.
  • Property Protection: We treat your lawn like our own, leaving the yard pristine.

Stump Grinding

Don’t let an old stump ruin your curb appeal or trip you up while you're mowing. We’ll grind it down fast so you can reclaim your yard.

  • Deep Extraction: We grind stumps well below the surface, chewing through major lateral roots.
  • Immediate Replanting: Leaves the ground flat and ready to plant fresh seed or turf like it was never there.
  • Machinery Protection: Eliminates hidden lawn hazards and prevents costly damage to your lawnmower blades.

Storm Damage

When Michigan weather gets rough, we’re the first call to make. We respond fast to clear downed trees and hazardous debris safely.

  • 24/7 Rapid Response: Quick mobilization to clear structural hazards, blocked driveways, and pathways.
  • Structure Mitigation: Advanced rigging to safely pluck heavy limbs off roofs without causing secondary damage.
  • Insurance Ready: We help document the damage cleanly to make your provider's claims process as smooth as possible.
