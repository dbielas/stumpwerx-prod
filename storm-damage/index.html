What’s Included

Fast Hazard Mitigation Without Secondary Damage.

Storm-damaged timber is under unpredictable tension. We lift and section heavy limbs off roofs, vehicles, and fences carefully—preventing further structural damage while clearing your property cleanly and documenting the work for your insurer.

Common Questions

Storm Damage FAQ

What should I do first if a tree hits my roof or power lines?

Keep everyone clear of the area—especially if downed power lines are involved (call DTE/your utility immediately to de-energize live lines). Once safe, call us for immediate hazard removal.

Will you help with documentation for homeowners insurance?

Yes. We take clear before-and-after photos and provide detailed, itemized invoices that match what Michigan insurance adjusters look for.

How far outside Macomb County do you travel for storm cleanup?

We respond across Macomb, Oakland, St. Clair, Lapeer, and Genesee counties—and frequently travel further for major storm events and large structural removals.
